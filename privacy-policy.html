Fallen Banner privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fallen Banner is a paid game for Android made by one independent developer. This page explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your saved games, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded by the game. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Feedback and crash reports

The pause menu has a Send feedback button, and if the game closed unexpectedly the title screen offers to send a crash report. Both only happen if you choose to. They open your own email app with a message already filled in, or copy the details for you to paste. You can read and change everything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press send yourself.

The details in a report are: the game version, where you were in the game, your hero's level and gold, your phone's model, Android version, screen size and graphics chip, the graphics setting you chose, and the last few error messages the game ran into. A report does not include your name, contacts, location or anything else from your phone. If you email a report, I also receive your email address, which I use only to reply to you and never share.

Google Play

The game is sold and installed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as purchase records and, if you have allowed it on your phone, crash and performance data that it shows to developers as anonymous statistics ("Android vitals"). That is handled by Google under the Google privacy policy, not by the game.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
